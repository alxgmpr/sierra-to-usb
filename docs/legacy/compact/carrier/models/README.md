# RF connector and eFuse models

`SMA_Amphenol_132289_EdgeMount.step` is the exact-part KiCad library model, copied unchanged from the installed KiCad 10 library on 2026-09-07. Upstream: https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries/kicad-packages3D/-/blob/master/Connector_Coaxial.3dshapes/SMA_Amphenol_132289_EdgeMount.step . KiCad library license: CC BY-SA 4.0 with the KiCad libraries exception; https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/ . This is KiCad library CAD, not a newly downloaded manufacturer model. Amphenol's direct CAD download requires login.

The original model's board stop is X=2.54 mm and its slot contact planes are Z=0 and Z=-1.73 mm. The custom footprint's origin is the board edge, so the model uses X=-2.54 mm offset, unit scale, no rotation. With the 1.50 mm carrier, the upper contacts sit on the top copper and the lower fork has 0.23 mm nominal solder clearance. Maximum nominal front projection is 12.70 mm (excluding geometric bounding tolerance). All five connectors use this model.

`TI_RPW0010A.wrl/.step` is a reconstructed package model generated by `../models.py` from TI drawing 4225183/A, 08/2019, in https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf , package-outline page 72. It represents the TPS259470LRPWR's nominal 2 x 2 mm body, 1 mm maximum height and ten exposed contacts, including the two full-length central power contacts. It is not manufacturer CAD. Mold draft, terminal corner radii and exact marking are omitted. Pin 1 is on the upper-left of the unrotated footprint. Placement is at unit scale with zero offset/rotation.

Other files retain their previously documented envelope status. The old SMA clearance block is retained as historical geometry but is no longer assigned to the PCB.

2026-09-07 simplification: the large heatsink, TIM, retention posts/frame and profile service-volume models are no longer generated or assigned. Their old standalone files are historical, unused assets. Adhesive cooling is intentionally unmodeled; J202 remains the standard 3M20-pin IDC header.
