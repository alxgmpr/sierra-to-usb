`check_capture.py` checks86 exported-net assertions, including invalid profile truth table, six isolated conflict contacts, source authorization, mux mapping, numeric power/control pins and four two-endpoint RF paths. Fourteen unit tests check selected independent numeric maps and generation-guard behavior. `check_board.py` independently compares all585 exported numeric pin/net entries and all159 components with the board. These checks detect capture/parity mistakes; none establishes hot electrical limits, USB compliance, RF performance or mechanical fit.

Custom footprint provenance is in `reference/carrier-land-patterns.json`; critical custom STEP envelope bounds are read back by `check_models.py`. Ordinary KiCad models are package representations, not supplier-certified models. USB and SIM imported CAD are secondary CAD data checked against the above drawings, not accepted solely because of library names.
