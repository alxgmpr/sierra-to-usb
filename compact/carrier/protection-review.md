# Connector protection and fault limits

D301 is Littelfuse SMF6.0A, with its cathode on raw USB_VBUS and anode on GND. Its6V standoff avoids normal5V tolerance conduction. The manufacturer specifies10.3V maximum clamp at19.4A for the stated10/1000µs pulse and400µA maximum25°C leakage at6V. This leakage is upstream of the current shunt and fits within the separate10mA upstream allowance. Hot leakage remains part of the current acceptance test. The footprint follows the manufacturer's1.3×1.4mm pads with1.6mm gap; the smaller generic KiCad pads were not used.

This TVS protects the raw28V-rated eFuse and ST VDD/VBUS inputs against connector transients. It **does not clamp the downstream buck input below6V**. U301 provides adjustable overvoltage shutoff; downstream capacitors absorb charge during its response. Require SENSED_VBUS below the6V absolute maximum of the buck and auxiliary LDO during the actual qualified hot-plug, detach and ESD tests. The eFuse's1.2µs typical OVLO response is not a guaranteed maximum or proof of system immunity. Normal operation is limited to the requested5V USB source; continuous erroneous high-voltage input is not an accepted operating mode. A shorted TVS relies on USB-source overcurrent protection and must fail the bring-up current check.

R308100Ω is in series with C302100nF, as TI recommends when dVdt capacitance exceeds10nF. At65nF effective minimum, TI's nominal slew relationship gives about0.0308V/ms. The maximum carrier input capacitance after the eFuse includes two22µF buck input capacitors and2.2µF LDO input capacitance. Taking positive initial and temperature tolerance gives approximately63.6µF and a nominal charging term below2mA, before always-on circuitry. This is separate from the later buck-output soft start. Verify eFuse slew extremes and CC behavior during slow AUX startup on hardware.

D101/D102 use TPD4EUSB30 for the four SuperSpeed pairs; D103 protects CC1/CC2 and the joined USB2 pair. Keep their returns at the receptacle and avoid branches on high-speed channels. USB2 and USB3 switches share USB_MUX_OFF and remain disabled through rail qualification and RUN delay. D203 protects module-supplied SIM signals; its10pF shunts are correctly paired with0805 C0G parts. Channel loading, ESD clamp behavior, asymmetric rail collapse and module back-power require system tests after routing.
